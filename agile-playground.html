Agile Playground

Learn Agile by doing.

Your team has about 65 minutes and a budget of S$3,000 to plan a 30th birthday party in Singapore for about 30 guests. You won't plan it all perfectly up front. You'll plan a little, show it, get feedback and adapt. That is Scrum.

3 accountabilitiesProduct Owner, Scrum Master, Developers
5 eventsThe Sprint, which contains Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review and Sprint Retrospective
3 artifacts + commitmentsProduct Backlog (Product Goal), Sprint Backlog (Sprint Goal), Increment (Definition of Done)

How to use this page: one person shares their screen and does the clicking; everyone talks. Go through the steps in order. Each step lights up its part of the Scrum framework map, tells you who leads it, and gives you a timer and a tool to fill in. Your work is saved automatically in this browser.
